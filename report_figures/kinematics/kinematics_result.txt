head joints    : head_roll, head_yaw, head_pitch, neck_pitch, jaw  (5 joints)
floating robot : 6 (free base) + 15 joints = 21 DOF
double support : legs + base form a closed loop: n = 10 (ground incl. feet, base, leg links), j = 10  ->  M = 6(10-1) - 5(10) = 4  (+5 head = 9)
single support : one leg = open chain of 5 joints -> the base can move in only 5 of its 6 directions relative to the stance foot (no ankle roll) -> sideways balance must come from hip_roll / stepping
